# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Talli contributors, maintainers, and community participants commit to a
respectful, harassment-free environment regardless of experience, identity,
background, disability, appearance, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion,
gender identity, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Accept correction and update claims when evidence changes.
- Respect privacy, especially around financial and security information.
- Use synthetic examples and redact accidental personal data immediately.
- Keep disagreements focused on the project's documented scope and evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention;
- personal attacks, sustained disruption, trolling, or intimidation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring anyone to reveal credentials or financial records; or
- knowingly submitting malicious code, fabricated evidence, or concealed risk.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or contributions that
violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, prior behavior, and
the safety of affected participants.

Public repository moderation tools may be used for conduct visible in public
threads. Sensitive conduct reports require a private maintainer channel.
**OWNER ACTION REQUIRED:** publish that private contact before making the
repository public. Security vulnerabilities should use the separate process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
